# ⚡ Divine Favor Powers

### Use Divine Favor Without Disabling Passive Powers

A **client-only Valheim mod** that changes how Divine Favor interacts with equipped Passive Powers from TalentTree and Passive Powers.

At maximum rank, **tap your Guardian Power key to activate the first equipped power, or hold it to rotate which equipped power comes first.**

Divine Favor no longer disables Passive Powers, and your existing hotkeys remain intact.

## ✨ Features

- **Tap to activate:** Tap your Guardian Power key to trigger the first equipped Passive Power.
- **Hold to rotate:** Hold the key to cycle which equipped power is first.
- **Passive Powers stay active:** Divine Favor no longer turns Passive Powers off.
- **Boss stones still work:** Use boss stones to equip and unequip powers as usual.
- **Other hotkeys remain unchanged:** Shift and the other Passive Powers hotkeys are left alone.
- **TalentTree cooldown reduction preserved:** TalentTree's cooldown reduction behavior remains unchanged.

## 📦 Requirements

Install the following mods:

- [BepInExPack for Valheim](https://thunderstore.io/c/valheim/p/denikson/BepInExPack_Valheim/)
- [TalentTree](https://thunderstore.io/c/valheim/p/M2Valheim/TalentTree/)
- [Passive Powers](https://thunderstore.io/c/valheim/p/Smoothbrain/PassivePowers/)

> **Client-side only:** Dedicated servers do not need this mod installed.

## 📥 Installation

### Thunderstore, Gale, or r2modman

Install the package through your mod manager and launch Valheim.

### Nexus Mods or Manual Installation

1. Download and extract the mod archive.
2. Copy the `plugins/cjayride-DivineFavorPowers` folder into your Valheim `BepInEx/plugins` directory.
3. Confirm the DLL is located at:

```text
Valheim/BepInEx/plugins/
└── cjayride-DivineFavorPowers/
    └── Cjayride.DivineFavorPowers.dll
```

**Do not copy** `manifest.json`, `README.md`, or `icon.png` into the game's plugin directory.

If you are packaging this mod yourself for Thunderstore, Hexium, or Nexus, replace `icon.png` with your own **256 × 256** icon before uploading.

## 🎮 How to Use

1. Equip your desired Passive Powers using the boss stones.
2. Reach maximum Divine Favor rank.
3. **Tap** the Guardian Power key to activate the first equipped power.
4. **Hold** the key to rotate the power order.

Shift and other Passive Powers hotkeys retain their normal behavior.
